# flipALB 🏚️➡️🏠
A map-first marketplace and data hub that makes vacant properties visible, actionable, and connected, linking rehabbers/flippers, the Albany County Land Bank, residents, homebuyers, nonprofits, and city policymakers around one shared problem: bringing empty homes back to life.

> Hackathon build: MVP in ~24 hours focused on mapping, property detail cards, interest leads, and resident reporting.

---

# Why this exists
Albany’s vacant homes are scattered across neighborhoods and siloed across systems. Meanwhile, investors can’t find reliable leads, residents don’t have an easy reporting channel, and public partners like the Land Bank need better prioritization signals.

# This app is the hub that routes one vacant property into multiple “activation paths”:
- rehab & flip
- Land Bank acquisition
- nonprofit affordable housing conversion
